Terms & Conditions

Clear and simple guidelines for parents and students.

1. Enrollment & Fees

We follow a simple monthly fee structure with no admission or registration charges. Enrollment is confirmed only after the student and parent attend the free trial class and decide to proceed.

2. Cancellation Policy

We do not require long-term commitments or annual contracts. Parents may discontinue enrollment at any time without a cancellation penalty.

3. Missed Classes & Rescheduling

If a student is unable to attend a class due to illness or school commitments, parents may request a rescheduled session by informing the academic coordinator at least 24 hours in advance.

4. Free Trial Class

Our trial class is completely free and comes with no obligation to enroll. No advance payment or commitment is required to book or attend a trial session.
