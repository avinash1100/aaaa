Privacy Policy

In compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 and IT Act 2000

Welcome to Aegis Learning Academy. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy outlines how we handle your information when you interact with our website.

1. Data Collection

To effectively manage free trial bookings and admission inquiries, we collect specific academic and contact details. This includes parent and student names, contact numbers, residential area, school and class details, preferred subjects, class mode, and any additional academic notes you choose to provide.

2. Data Processing & Security

Your data is used solely for the purpose of academic coordination and scheduling. Information submitted through our forms is securely routed to our internal management systems. For your convenience, basic details are used to generate WhatsApp communication links to connect you directly with our Academic Director.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We use trusted external services (such as Google Fonts and Font Awesome) to deliver necessary design elements on our website. These essential technical services do not actively collect personal user data through our platform. Our Aegis Insights page embeds YouTube videos, and playing an embedded video may set cookies or collect usage data under Google's privacy policy.

4. Tracking & Analytics

We believe in a clean, straightforward browsing experience. Our website operates without any tracking cookies, marketing pixels, or third-party web analytics tools.
